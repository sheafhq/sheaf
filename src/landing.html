Verifiable evidence for what an AI agent actually did.

Sheaf intercepts MCP tool calls at the boundary, signs every event outside the agent process, hash-chains the record, and anchors Merkle roots on Base L2. The resulting receipt verifies offline — no Sheaf server required.

A .sheaf file, or it didn't happen.

In a perfect world, you could trust the receipt you get from a vendor or service provider. You wouldn't need to worry about edited logs, mutable databases, or changing model versions. But AI agents act at machine speed, and application logs are designed for debugging, not for legal disputes. Sheaf records each tool call at the boundary, outside the agent, and gives you a portable file you can check yourself, without trusting the operator's logs or our servers. What it does not prove is written down too.
